## Motion

Drawer/Sheet content travels in from its `side` by default while the backdrop
fades. Set `className` on `DrawerContent` and use `data-side`, `data-state`, and
`data-presence` to replace the directional CSS; Combric still owns focus,
keyboard behavior, interruption, and final unmount. Reduced motion is
automatic. See the
[Motion Foundation](/docs/v1.3.1/foundations/motion/).
