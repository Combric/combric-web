## Motion

Tooltip content fades and translates/scales by default and is removed after its
visual exit. Customize or locally disable the presentation through
`TooltipContent`'s `className` and public state/side attributes. Reduced motion
is automatic. See the
[Motion Foundation](/docs/v1.2.0/foundations/motion/).
