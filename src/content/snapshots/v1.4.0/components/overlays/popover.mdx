## Motion

Popover content fades and translates/scales by default, with presence-aware
cleanup. Customize or locally disable it through `PopoverContent`'s `className`
and public state/side attributes. Reduced motion is automatic. See the
[Motion Foundation](/docs/v1.4.0/foundations/motion/).
