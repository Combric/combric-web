## Motion

Toast fades and translates/scales on entry and exit by default. `Toast` accepts
the existing `className` hook for custom CSS or a local `transition: none`
opt-out; its logical close and live-region semantics remain unchanged. Reduced
motion is automatic. See the
[Motion Foundation](/docs/v1.4.0/foundations/motion/).
